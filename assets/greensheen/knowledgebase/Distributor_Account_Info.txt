📦 Becoming a GreenSheen Distributor

To apply for a distributor account:
- Visit: www.greensheenpaints.com/distributors/apply
- Provide business license, resale certificate, and tax ID
- Account approval typically takes 3–5 business days

Distributor Benefits:
- Bulk pricing and volume discounts
- Dedicated account manager
- Early access to new eco-product lines
- Co-branded marketing materials

Contact: trade@greensheenpaints.com
